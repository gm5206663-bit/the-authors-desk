THE AUTHOR'S DESK

The control room for a twelve-repository account. Three things live here and only here: the desk (every decision that is the author's to make), the register (every book and chapter-bearing tree, measured), and the records (the dated close notes of every operation).
twelve repositories · all public register: 24 books · 377 chapters · 1,624,963 words counts regenerated, never typed
One copy of one thing — a document kept in two places will be wrong in one of them. Counts are measured, never typed. Everything public: no archived repositories, no private state, no hidden corners.
