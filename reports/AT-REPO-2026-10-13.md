## Why this repository exists

The Adaptation Talent is the account's signature ability system, and its doctrine had
accumulated where it was written: inside a dozen serial foundations, a codex, panels, and
logs — spread across the kit, stark_heir, and mcu-eternal-fanfic. This repository is its
**organized reading home**: one place where the law, its holders, the craft, and the
provenance sit in order.
