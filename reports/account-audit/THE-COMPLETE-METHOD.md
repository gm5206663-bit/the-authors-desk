# PART 1 · THE CORE

## 1.1 The core belief ⬛ UNIVERSAL

> **Build a coherent living world → put characters in it → simulate what would really happen → convert that simulation into prose without breaking established truth.**

It is not "write an exciting scene." Priority order when things conflict:

```
Consistency      > convenience
Causality        > coincidence
Character agency > author control
Earned progression > arbitrary escalation
Consequences     > spectacle
Truth            > memory
```

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/03_STORY_LAW.md` §1

## 1.2 The Prime Law ⬛ UNIVERSAL

> **The OC is the ACTOR, never the SPECTATOR.**
> Verb-count test: if the protagonist only *watches*, *files* and *notes*, the chapter is broken.

**Source:** `library/FANFICTION_FRAMEWORK.md` §2

## 1.3 The eight framework laws ⬛ UNIVERSAL

| # | Law | Statement |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **PRIME** | The OC is the actor, never the spectator |
| 2 | **CALIBRATION** | No sandbag fights. Escalating ladder, strain shown. Beating a weaker opponent proves nothing |
| 3 | **IDENTITY** | The OC's core is an identity, not a toolkit. Strip the auxiliary abilities — can the core still win? |
| 4 | **PERSONALITY** | Give them a *voice*. Teasing, humour, warmth — that is what makes quiet moments land |
| 5 | **BUTTERFLY-INTEGRATION** | Canon characters must react to the OC. Never reproduce a canon beat verbatim while pretending the OC isn't in the room |
| 6 | **GROWTH** | Every progression line moves every chapter, or the stillness is explicitly justified |
| 7 | **ENSEMBLE** | The supporting cast is the spine. If a character stops appearing, the story must say why |
| 8 | **NO CHAPTER WITHOUT VERIFIED CANON** | If canon can't be read, say so and stop. Never invent and label it canonical |

**Source:** `library/FANFICTION_FRAMEWORK.md` §2

## 1.4 The five failures ⬛ UNIVERSAL — *the expensive lesson*

Every one was committed in a real serial, at length, and each was **invisible from the inside** until a reader said the word "nonsense."

| Failure | Symptom | Cure |
|---|---|---|
| **No canon spine** | Nothing from the source world actually happens on the page | Name the canon beats you touch. Five minimum |
| **Nothing contested** | No antagonist, no rival, no test. The protagonist's only verb is "keep" | Every chapter gets a threat and a face |
| **No felt progression** | Ranks, rings and skills never move | Rank gates are structure. Use them |
| **One register repeated** | Every chapter reads the same | Five registers, ≥3 per chapter |
| **Apparatus outgrew the story** | Panels and ledgers longer than scenes | Slim panel. Bookkeeping lives in the kit |

**Diagnostic order when a reader says "nonsense":** do not re-word it. Find **what it skipped**. Walk all five. **If more than two are true, you are not editing — you are rebuilding.** Say so plainly and do it.

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/03_STORY_LAW.md` §2, §7

## 1.5 The per-chapter contract ⬛ UNIVERSAL

Every chapter must satisfy all seven. Check **before** drafting, not after.

```
1. CANON BEAT   — what canon does this touch, and how does it stay true?
2. THREAT       — what can hurt someone here?
3. FACE         — who opposes, named, with dialogue?
4. MOVEMENT     — what changes: rank, ring, skill, bond, resource, knowledge?
5. BUTTERFLY    — what does the protagonist's existence change about canon?
6. REGISTERS    — at least 3 of the five
7. TURN         — what is true at the end that was not true at the start?
```

> **A chapter that is only interior meditation fails and does not ship.** Hard rule, not a guideline.

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/03_STORY_LAW.md` §3

## 1.6 The five decisions you cannot defer ⬛ UNIVERSAL

| # | Decision | The trap |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Which era?** | Pick one and stay in it |
| 2 | **Who is the protagonist — and what are they NOT?** | The commonest failure is an OC who quietly acquires a bloodline, a system, a past life, or a canon character's private mechanic. Decide the ceiling now |
| 3 | **Where does the story touch canon?** | Name specific beats. If you can't name five, your story is not set in that world — it is set *next to* it |
| 4 | **What is the spine — who is hunting your protagonist?** | *"\_\_\_\_ wants \_\_\_\_ from my protagonist, and will \_\_\_\_ to get it."* If you can't fill all three blanks, stop |
| 5 | **What will you never do?** | Write the absolutes before you need them, because the moment you need them is the moment you'll be tempted |

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/00_START_HERE.md`

## 1.7 The twelve locks ⬛ UNIVERSAL

Set **before** drafting. Write into `NO_MISTAKE_LIVE_RULES.md`. Anything you cannot answer now will be answered **by accident** later — and the accident will be in chapter forty.

```
 1. ERA            — which series, which years, which range
 2. PROTAGONIST    — who, and explicitly what they are NOT
 3. CANON ENTRY    — the specific canon beats this serial touches (name ≥5)
 4. SPINE          — who wants what from the protagonist, and what they'll do
 5. POWER CEILING  — the strongest the OC becomes, and what they never get
 6. IDENTITY       — public name vs true name; what the reveal costs
 7. ABSOLUTES      — what the protagonist will never do, ever
 8. CANON IMMUNITY — which canon characters' fates may not be rerouted
 9. MEASUREMENT    — how rank and power are shown on the page
10. VOICE          — POV, tense, register rules
11. CADENCE        — chapters per pass, words per chapter, when audits run
12. HANDOFF        — what a fresh agent must read first
```

**Lock 4 is the one that matters most.** Blue Silver shipped **90,000 words without one.** The rebuild's entire engine was filling that sentence in.

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/02_PROJECT_SETUP.md`

## 1.8 Causality, not coincidence ⬛ UNIVERSAL

Every important beat answers three questions:

> **Why does this happen? Why now? Why this way?**

If you cannot answer all three, the beat is not ready. Every divergence propagates:

```
trigger → immediate effect → secondary effect → character reaction
        → institutional reaction → long-term consequence
```

> **A rule that is never broken is not a butterfly** — it changes nothing downstream. If your divergence changes nothing, do not claim it changed the story.

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/03_STORY_LAW.md` §4

## 1.9 The living world ⬛ UNIVERSAL

While the protagonist does X, everyone else is doing something. The world functions when the protagonist is absent.

- **Pressure items age.** A debt unpaid in chapter 3 is worse in chapter 12
- **Silence is a decision.** Someone not answering is a choice, not a gap
- **An honest "not now" beats accidental forgetting.** If a thread is parked, park it deliberately and say so in the ledger

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/03_STORY_LAW.md` §5

# PART 2 · PROSE AND VOICE

## 2.1 The Language Law ⬛ UNIVERSAL

> **English only. No characters in a script the reader cannot read — CJK ideographs, kana, hangul — in any file: prose, panels, ledgers, notes.**

**This is not a blanket non-ASCII ban.** Em dashes, en dashes, curly quotes, `≈` and the `◆` panel marker are correct, portable typography and are **explicitly allowed**. The proven serial this kit was distilled from carries **289 em dashes and zero CJK characters.**

> The distinction matters because **an over-broad rule gets quietly ignored, and a rule that is ignored stops being a rule.** Ban the thing that actually breaks — unreadable script — and the check stays honest.

Names are written in **pinyin**: A Yin, Tang San, Lan Xuanyu. Never in characters.

Two artifacts to never emit:
- **Literal backslash-n sequences.** Never write that two-character escape sequence anywhere — *including in rules about it*, which is how it keeps coming back. Express it in a script as `chr(92) + 'n'`
- **Placeholder or template text** left in a shipped file

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/07_PROSE_LAW.md` §1

## 2.2 The five registers ⬛ UNIVERSAL

Fifteen chapters in one register is unreadable however good each chapter is. **Every chapter uses at least three.**

| Register | What it is | Example use |
|---|---|---|
| **Close sense** | Immediate physical experience, inside a body or a place | The pressure of a beast's cultivation through the ground |
| **Hard scene** | Real-time action with consequence | A fight, a hunt, an escape, a measurement |
| **Dialogue** | Spoken exchange between named characters | An antagonist explaining what they want |
| **Other POV** | A scene from someone else's eyes, usually italicised | The hunter's report; the clerk reading a file |
| **Lyrical** | Elevated, reflective, time-compressed | The long view; what the years did |

> The **dialogue** register is the one that goes missing, because interior protagonists don't talk and introspective prose is easier to write. Its absence is detectable and fatal: a serial with no spoken exchange has no faces, and a serial with no faces has no opposition.

**Hard gate: every chapter carries at least three spoken dialogue lines.** If your protagonist cannot speak, give the chapter to someone who can.

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/07_PROSE_LAW.md` §2

## 2.3 Panel discipline ⬛ UNIVERSAL

**Slim panel at the top. No footer.**

```
◆ STATUS — Chapter N: <year range>
KIND      : <what the protagonist is, right now>
AGE       : <age>
CLASS     : <rank / tier, and what just opened>
THREAT    : <this chapter's opposition>
BOND      : <only if the chapter turns on a relationship>
LOSS      : <only if something ends>
LAW       : <only if a rule is established or tested>
```

That is it. **Not eleven fields.** The bookkeeping — position, canon touched, world changes, wires left open — lives in `SERIAL_LOG.md`, where a reader never sees it.

> **A footer in the chapter file is apparatus outgrowing story.** It is failure mode five and it is revoked.

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/07_PROSE_LAW.md` §3

## 2.4 The marker law ⬛ UNIVERSAL

`◆` **marks apparatus.** It belongs to the panel and nowhere else in prose.

The single exception is a **book-end card** — a fenced block closing a *book*, not a chapter:

```
◆ END OF BOOK ONE — <BOOK TITLE>
<chapter count>. <span>. <the one thing the book was about>.
<any standing count the reader has been tracking>.
```

**Three short lines.** If you need more than three, you are writing a footer and calling it a card.

Gate enforcement: a `◆` in prose fails. Two cards fail. A card that does not open with `END OF` fails. A card that is not last fails.

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/07_PROSE_LAW.md` §3

## 2.5 The Number Law ⬛ UNIVERSAL

```
PANELS and LEDGERS  → exact figures permitted
PROSE               → round felt counts, in words, spoken or thought by characters
```

A character thinks *he had lived a long time*. The ledger says 743.

> **Prose carries no digits at all.** Not years, not ranks, not distances. This is a hard gate and it is machine-checked. It forces the prose to express magnitude as experience rather than as bookkeeping — **the difference between a story and a spreadsheet.**

Sole exception: a number a character literally reads off a document or device, in dialogue or quoted text — but it must be **in words**, and justified as something being read.

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/07_PROSE_LAW.md` §4 · `05_POWER_LAW.md` §3

## 2.6 Voice Law ⬛ UNIVERSAL

Lock in setup and never drift:

- **POV** — whose head are we in? Can it change? How is a change marked?
- **Tense** — past or present, and never both
- **Person** — first or third
- **Register floor** — see §2.2

> A POV change that isn't marked is a continuity error **the reader feels before they can name it.**

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/07_PROSE_LAW.md` §5

## 2.7 The full-play / story economy law ⬛ UNIVERSAL

Every element justifies the narrative space it consumes.

**Never:** thin hinge chapters that only move the cast somewhere · copy-machine rooms (the same scene type repeated with different nouns) · kit-recap presented as story · audits presented as story · a chapter whose only content is interior meditation.

**Always:** play full — use the kit, the cast, the consequences · a named job finishes in the turn it is given · if a character is introduced, they want something **in the scene they arrive in**.

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/07_PROSE_LAW.md` §6 · `03_STORY_LAW.md` §6

## 2.8 Filename Law ⬛ UNIVERSAL

```
ASCII letters, digits, underscores, dots. Nothing else.
No spaces. No special characters. No non-ASCII.
```

`Chapter_07_The_Teeth_in_the_Grass.md` — yes · `Chapter 7 — The Teeth in the Grass.md` — no

> Upload-safe names survive transfer between agents, platforms and archives. Pretty names get mangled, and **a mangled filename in a handoff is a lost file.**

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/07_PROSE_LAW.md` §7

## 2.9 HOUSE GRAMMAR — the measured standard ⬛ UNIVERSAL

*Measured 2026-09-20 against published fanfiction read directly and against the workspace corpus.* A chapter outside this band **does not read like the fandom** and will be rejected regardless of its facts.

- **Sentence average ≤ 25 words; no sentence over 60; median near 15**
- **Dialogue ≥ 8–10 spoken lines per thousand words** in any chapter that has people in it. Exposition is delivered in speech, by named characters with wants
- **Open inside a scene** (a body doing something, or a voice speaking)
- **Several small finished scenes** per chapter, at least one in real time
- **Plain concrete nouns.** Motif words are texture, never the sentence engine
- **End on a hook**: a decision, an arrival, a threat, a name

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/07_PROSE_LAW.md` §9

## 2.10 SCOPE LAW ⬛ UNIVERSAL

*Author, verbatim:* **"Why you making nonsense by writing nonsenses like it becomes boring, wyrite what needs to write not everything, you can skip"**

1. A beat is written **once**. No paragraph explains a paragraph; no chapter re-tells its own events at the close
2. **No overview openings** (season summaries, status reports). Open in scene
3. **One line of consequence** after a scene; never a summary of the scene
4. **Budget: 2,400–3,000 words a chapter**; up to 3,400 only for a chapter that carries a season's turn, reason recorded
5. **Skip what changes nothing**: repeated readings, repeated lessons, scenery that carries neither. **Cutting removes explanation, never the scene**
6. Every rule above still stands over it

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/07_PROSE_LAW.md` §10

## 2.11 PLAIN LANGUAGE LAW ⬛ UNIVERSAL

*Author, verbatim:* **"What the hell even this writeing style what you can't write clear that can be understood, why this poem type nonsenses"**

1. Narration says what happened in **plain words**; a stranger must understand every sentence **on one reading**
2. **No invented code nouns as narration.** If a sentence only makes sense to someone who knows the serial's private vocabulary, rewrite it
3. In-world names (teams, lists, lines) may appear **only in speech**, and only where the scene explains them
4. The subject of a sentence is a thing or a body; **ideas never carry it**
5. Check before shipping: grep the retired word list. Any hit in narration is a **defect, not a style choice**
6. Stands **over** the house grammar and the scope law; it never excuses a scene being cut or a voice being thinned

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/07_PROSE_LAW.md` §11

## 2.12 SENTENCE-LEVEL DNA — the voice bar ⬛ UNIVERSAL

*Measured 2026-09-20 per 1,000 words of prose body (panels, headers, tables, footers excluded).*

| Marker | House bar | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **negation** (did not / was not / had not) | **17.3** | the house's most-used construction |
| **remembered / forgot** (body & object memory) | **3.8** | *the signature* — see below |
| **"the way X does Y when Z" / "as if"** | **5.1** | |
| **"a X, not a Y"** (definitional equation) | **2.1** | |
| **noun-phrase fragments** ("Pockets. Cheap.") | **5.1** | flat filing rhythm, in the POV's room-scan |
| **exclamation marks** | **0** | the house does not shout |
| **question marks** | **0** | interrogatives take a full stop |
| **em dashes** | **1.3** | above ~5 = leaning on the dash instead of the sentence |
| **plain `said`** as speech tag | **14.3** | **tags are boring on purpose** — emotion is carried by the action beat around them, never by the verb |

### The signature construction — remembered / forgot

> **`[body part or object] + [remembered / forgot / found] + [the thing it is still holding]`**

- *"Oscar's stomach remembered it had a job."*
- *"The wood under Yin Shu's boots remembered a different weight."*
- *"Yin Shu's clipboard forgot what it was for."*
- *"Dashi's feet forgot they had a job."*
- *"Flender's mouth found something that had not been there when a prince had said lousy."*

**Used for reaction, never for exposition.** It puts memory and attention into **bodies and objects instead of the POV character's head** — which is how the house gets interiority without interior monologue.

**Source:** `lan_shen/foundation/STYLE_LAW.md` §0, §0.5

## 2.13 THE TWELVE STYLE PATTERNS ⬛ UNIVERSAL — *the gold, consulted not stamped*

*Distilled 2026-09-02 from 92 chapters. Consult before writing any chapter.*

| # | Pattern | Deploy rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Question-Title** — titles that are themes, not labels | title = the chapter's one-sentence answer, unknown until the end |
| 2 | **Arithmetic as Soul** — interior prices the world; emotion becomes cost, cost becomes grammar | any strong beat may pass through **one** price-clause. Never two in a row |
| 3 | **The Grain-Read** — the talent's voice without a voice; the world read as material | **extend, never repeat** — each extension must read a **new class** of thing |
| 4 | **The Drawer** — a motif with a life, literal → figurative → institutional | once per 8–12 chapters, **evolved one step**. Never name what's in it |
| 5 | **The First Hand In** — brother grammar; love is in logistics | show the hand's position **before** the event; let the event arrive |
| 6 | **The Hall That Hears Everything** — institutions as quiet listeners | for consequence beyond the room. **Vary the institution, never the construction twice unchanged** |
| 7 | **The Perspective Panels** — the world seen side-on through strangers who owe the protagonist nothing | one panel per arc-stretch; **the stranger must gain something from having watched** |
| 8 | **Cliff Rhythm** — alternate short and long; grammar mirrors event size | after a long lyrical cliff, take a short one next; end on **process** when the threat is offstage |
| 9 | **The Callback-Object Economy** — objects carry history and pay it back | **every arc must spend one old object and bank one new one** |
| 10 | **The Not-Irony Tag** — deadpan measurement humour, never cruel | one tag per scene maximum; **the tag belongs to the WATCHER, not the speaker** |
| 11 | **The Refrain, Then the Break** — a three-word law repeated until breaking it IS the development | only **one** refrain live at a time; the break must cost something on-page |
| 12 | **The Ongoing Image** — continuity expressed as weather, water, sound | when the chapter's feeling is too large for any character, **give it to the setting** |

**The shape of the whole (measured):** 92 chapters · avg 3,078 words (band ~1,400–5,000) · dialogue 5.5%–66.6% (avg 24.5%).

> **The dialogue dial is a lens, not a default.** Lesson-chapters ran 44–66%; hush-chapters 8–15%; recovery chapters 39–42%. **Choose the chapter's dialogue load like a camera chooses its lens.**

**Law (oo-2): the gold is consulted, not decorated with** — one or two patterns breathing per chapter beats a checklist stamped whole.

**Source:** `reference/sl3_lin_hao/Soul_Land_3_Project/STYLE_GOLD.md`

## 2.14 The prose audit ⬛ UNIVERSAL

```
- Zero characters in an unreadable script (CJK, kana, hangul) — not a blanket non-ASCII ban
- Zero literal backslash-n sequences
- Zero digits in prose
- At least three spoken dialogue lines
- At least three of the five registers present

# PART 3 · CANON, CONTINUITY AND POWER

## 3.1 The confidence tags 🟦 SOUL LAND (method is ⬛ UNIVERSAL)

- **`[canon]`** — verified against source material or multiple independent secondary sources
- **`[fan]`** — widely repeated, plausible, *not verified*. Verify before building a plot on it
- **`[disputed]`** — sources disagree. Pick a reading, record it, and **never switch silently**
- **`[design]`** — your invention on top of canon. **Must be labelled as yours, never as canon**

> **Never present a `[fan]` or `[design]` claim as `[canon]` in prose or in a status file. The distinction is the whole discipline.**

**The provenance rule:** `[canon]` means *multiple independent secondary sources agree* — **not** *read in the original text*. That is a weaker guarantee and you should know it before you build a plot on one of those rows.

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/01_CANON_SPINE.md`

## 3.2 The Canon-Butterfly Law ⬛ UNIVERSAL

> **Canon is not a list of events to mention in a footer.**

A canon scene must be written with its causes, its emotional pressure, its characters' adult motives, its public/private boundary, and its consequences. **If you can only summarise a canon beat, you have not written it — you have filed it.**

Equally: your OC is **not** inserted into every private canon scene. But when they are naturally present in a public or shared scene, their existence **must change something** — reactions, records, choices, timing, or emotional residue.

> **Do not suppress natural butterfly effects to protect a prewritten outline.**
> **Do not force butterfly effects where your OC has no access.**

### The eight questions — for every canon chapter you touch

```
1. What exactly happens in canon?
2. Who is present?
3. What is public information?
4. What is private or family-only?
5. What emotion or pressure drives the scene?
6. What changes after the scene?
7. Where can your OC naturally touch this?
8. Where must your OC NOT intrude?
```

### The seven butterfly categories — *use these instead of random changes*

| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Presence** | OC physically present in a public scene, changing how people react |
| **Absence** | OC's absence, or another's, changes routine |
| **Relationship** | A friendship or rivalry changes messages, worry, choices, interpretation |
| **Record** | Institutions record the OC differently because they exist under that file |
| **Resource** | Family money, access and status shape available options |
| **Training** | A lesson learned earlier changes an action later |
| **Environmental** | The setting leaves body-level or circumstance-level effects |

> **The `record` category is the most underused and the most powerful.** Institutions write things down. A report filed in chapter five, read by a clerk in chapter fifteen, acted on in chapter thirty — that is canon-shaped consequence **with no combat required**.

### What butterflies must NEVER do

- Replace the canon protagonist
- Steal a canon character's private emotional pillars
- Hand your OC canon secrets they did not earn
- Break canon events purely for drama
- Put your OC where logistics say they would not be
- Turn a talent or system into an all-purpose solution
- Make your OC's power override higher canon bloodlines **by assertion**

### The parallel rule — *the discipline that separates "a story set in Soul Land" from "Soul Land rewritten around me"*

> **Canon runs as itself. Your OC lives inside it.** The world does not centre on them. If a canon event happens off-page and your OC never hears about it, **that is correct and it should stay that way** — the reader can see it in parallel prose without your OC knowing.

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/04_CANON_BUTTERFLY.md` · `PANEL_BUTTERFLY_DOCTRINE.md`

## 3.3 The Butterfly Law — the sharpest form ⬛ UNIVERSAL

> **A butterfly effect is not a decoration added on top of a canon scene. It is the reason the scene is different at all. If a canon scene can be dropped into the story unchanged, the butterfly effect has not been applied — the story is canon with an extra character standing in it.**

- **Effect without its cause shown on-page is not a butterfly; it is a claim**
- Canon's superlatives must be **scoped**: any line making someone "the strongest/best/highest" in a group the OC belongs to must survive his presence. *(The founding failure: copying canon's "Xie Xie's soul power is the highest" into a class containing the OC — that is transcription, and it erases the OC from his own story)*

**Source:** `PANEL_BUTTERFLY_DOCTRINE.md` LAW 1

## 3.4 The Perspective Panel Doctrine ⬛ UNIVERSAL

1. **Every chapter stages at least one panel belonging to someone who is not the OC** — and the panel's scene must be **checked out of canon itself**
2. **A panel with no canon chapter behind it is an AU invention and must be labelled AU.** A chapter built mostly of such inventions is the wrong chapter
3. **The butterfly test extends to every character**, not just the OC. The cause → effect chain is written panel by panel
4. **Divergences are an economy** — a ledger of standing changes to initial conditions, each with magnitude (**dust · current · bend · break · STORM**), status, horizon, and last-paid. Both directions must derive: **no forced canon, no forced divergence**
5. **Use everything:** canon episodes, ensemble states, locks, **minor POVs** (an examiner, a kitchen elder, an apprentice, a market vendor, a letter-writer), panels, letters, machines. **The world is full of people who do not know your OC exists — show them**
6. **Locks still govern.** Another character's panel may *approach* a locked door but **never open it**

### Law 3 — Legend accumulates in outside voices
The "legend" grows in *other people's* observations, **never in his own narration.** One such panel per era.

### Law 4 — Canon is ore, not fate
World-facts are true until touched; canon's plot is **one timeline's path**. When your state implies a different outcome, **yours wins and is derived, never snapped back.**

**Source:** `PANEL_BUTTERFLY_DOCTRINE.md`

## 3.5 Knowledge Firewalls ⬛ UNIVERSAL

> **Standing rule: old trust stays real. Private secrets stay protected. Public consequences must breathe.**

### The four layers

```
1. HARD TRUTH          — verified canon, universe rules, explicit user decisions, established on-page facts
2. APPROVED DIVERGENCE — user-approved changes from source
3. STORY STATE         — everything that has happened on the page
4. CHARACTER KNOWLEDGE — what each person knows, believes, misreads, or has never heard
```

**Layer 4 is the one that breaks.** Layers 1–3 are about the world. **Layer 4 is about minds, and minds are not synchronised.**

> **Knowledge firewall: a character never behaves as if they know what they haven't learned.**

### What counts as earned

A secret is earned by: being **told** on page · being **witnessed** directly · being **deduced** from information the character actually holds, with the reasoning shown · being **recorded** somewhere they have legitimate access.

A secret is **not** earned by: the author needing them to know · the reader knowing it · the character being smart · "they would obviously have figured it out."

> **That last one is the trap. Smart characters still cannot know things they have no route to.**

### The breathing rule

**Public facts cause public consequences.** If the OC's rank is measured in an official assessment, the institution records it, people react, and opportunities change. Suppressing that to protect a plot is the anti-nerf violation.

**Private facts stay private.** They leak when someone earns them — never because the plot needs them to.

> The tension between those two rules is where most of the good drama lives.

### The firewall audit

```
- Does any character act on information they have no route to?
- Does any character FAIL TO REACT to something they definitely know?
- Did a public event fail to produce a public consequence?
- Did a private fact leak without an earned route?
- Is the firewall file updated?
```

> **The second question is as important as the first.** A firewall is not just about secrets leaking — it is about known things being *used*. A character who knows a fact and never lets it affect a decision **has been written as a prop.**

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/06_KNOWLEDGE_FIREWALLS.md`

## 3.6 Power Law ⬛ UNIVERSAL

### The anti-nerf rule

> **Never reduce your protagonist's power to preserve a canon outcome.**

This is binding, not decorative. It produces the most reader distrust, because **the reader can feel the author's hand.**

If your OC is publicly stronger than canon expects, then **public facts cause public consequences**: paperwork changes, institutions pay attention, training allocation shifts, trial eligibility moves, rivalries recalibrate.

When your OC's power becomes public, ask:
```
- What does the institution record now?
- Who is threatened by this?
- What resource or opportunity opens or closes?
- Which canon character must now treat the OC differently?
```
> If none of those change, **you have nerfed by omission.** Go back.

### The other direction — no unearned power

Every gain answers five questions:
```
1. What changed?
2. What caused it?
3. What did they already possess that made this possible?
4. What failed first?
5. What limitation remains?
```
> **Question 4 is the one everyone skips.** A gain that cost nothing and overcame nothing is not progression, it is a gift from the author.

> **Nothing is absolute.** Rank and title labels are *estimates about a fight*, not fixtures. A lower-ranked character with the right counter beats a higher-ranked one, and the reader should be able to see why afterward.

### Measurement parity

- **One measurement source.** `STATUS_PANEL.md` states current rank and power. Nothing else does
- **Public and private values may differ — record both.** A beast recorded at 100 years publicly and 480 privately is fine and is a plot device. A beast that is 100 in one file and 480 in another with no explanation **is an error**
- **Every number has a source.** A rank that appears in prose came from a measurement, an assessment, or a document. **Say which, in the ledger**

### The ring gate 🟦 SOUL LAND — *but the principle is universal*

Soul Land hands you a progression system that is *already dramatic*: you cannot pass a multiple of ten without a soul ring. That means:

- Every tenth level is a **decision point** with real stakes
- Every ring requires **killing something** — a moral event, not a menu selection
- Every ring's colour tells the reader **what it cost** (white 10 / yellow 100 / purple 1,000 / black 10,000 / red 100,000)
- Every ring is **public information** — people can see your rings, and they will judge you

> **Use all four properties. A ring acquisition is the natural centre of a chapter: the hunt, the kill, the absorption, the reaction.** A serial that never shows a rank move, a ring taken, or a skill earned **has thrown away the franchise's best gift.**

### No plot armour

```
- What could be lost here?      If the answer is nothing, the scene has no stakes
- Who could fail?               If nobody can, the scene has no tension
- Why isn't this easy?          If you cannot answer, it should be easy — and then it should be over quickly
- What did it cost?             A victory that cost nothing reads as a gift
```

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/05_POWER_LAW.md`

## 3.7 Continuity Law ⬛ UNIVERSAL

### The law

> **Set the panel endpoints for a chapter FIRST. Then write every in-chapter date against them. Never the reverse.**

Writing dates as prose and reconciling afterwards is how a finished serial shipped **seventeen arithmetic errors and one hard canon violation** — the finale landed 49 years after a canon character had already left the story, **which made the ending impossible as written.**

> Not one of those errors was hard to fix. **All of them were invisible from inside the draft.** That is exactly why the order matters.

### The procedure

```
1. Open CONTINUITY.md and read the anchor table
2. Decide the chapter's start year and end year. Write them in the panel
3. Check the end year against every canon constraint that bounds this story
4. Only then write the chapter, deriving every in-chapter date from the endpoints
5. After drafting, re-derive every date in the prose and check it against the panel
6. Add the new anchors to the table
```

> **Step 3 is the one that saves the serial.** Checking the constraint before drafting instead of after would have cost one line instead of a five-chapter compression.

### The anchor table — *the most valuable single artifact in the kit*

```
| Anchor               | Year | Chapter | Note       |
|----------------------|------|---------|------------|
| First hunting party  |    1 |       1 |            |
| Mantis arrives       |   61 |       3 |            |
| The killing          |   98 |       3 | founds the mercy law |
| Mantis dies          |  104 |       3 | 61 + 43    |
```

> **Note the last row carries its own arithmetic. Write the derivation into the table and the error becomes visible the moment you type it.**

**Panel contiguity check:** chapter panels must be contiguous — **no overlap, no gap.** Machine-checked. Catches whole classes of drift, including chapters inserted or reordered without updating their neighbours.

### One source of truth

`STATUS_PANEL.md` is the **only** file that states current state. Everything else derives.

> **The moment two files both claim to be current, one is stale and nobody knows which.** This is not hypothetical — it is the failure that produces the long tail of small contradictions readers find and authors cannot.

> **Old numbers get corrected at the source, not whispered around the project.** If the panel says one thing and a codex file says another, **fix the codex file. Do not add a note explaining the difference.** Notes explaining contradictions accumulate until the project has more reconciliation text than story.

### Forward references

Every "X years later," "she would not learn until," and "in time he would understand" is a **promise**.

```
| Promise                | Made in | Paid off in | Status |
|------------------------|---------|-------------|--------|
| "in forty-seven years" |     15  |  (external) | MATCH  |
```

> **Compute them.** "In forty-seven years" from year 753 must land on a year that means something. The first draft said "two hundred and eleven years" and pointed **past the end of the series.**
> **Pay them off or cut them.** An unpaid forward reference is a debt.

### Supersession — *an unstamped stale file is a trap with a five-minute fuse*

When a draft is replaced, **stamp the old files.** Do not delete them — they are history and the receipts still matter — but mark them unmistakably:

```
<!-- ============================================================
     SUPERSEDED — DO NOT USE AS CONTINUITY
     The authoritative continuity is: foundation/CONTINUITY.md
     Live chapters: chapters_rebuilt/
     Dates, ages and character state here are STALE.
     ============================================================ -->
```

> **Classify before stamping.** A blanket stamp over files containing still-binding canon law **destroys valid reference.** Sort them:
> - **State mirrors** (status panels, timelines, character dossiers) → stamp superseded
> - **Mixed law + state** (world bibles, doctrine files) → **qualified banner**: law binds, state rows are stale
> - **Pure law** (canon receipts, prose law, research rules) → **leave alone**

### The continuity audit

```
- Does every in-chapter date match the panel endpoints?
- Is the panel contiguous with its neighbours (no overlap, no gap)?
- Is every new anchor added to the table?
- Does any date violate a canon constraint?
- Are all forward references computed and tracked?
- Is there exactly one file claiming to state current state?
```

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/08_CONTINUITY_LAW.md`

## 3.8 THE TWO-COPIES LAW ⬛ UNIVERSAL — *the root cause of nearly every recurring defect*

When the same fact is written in two places, **one will drift.** The law has four rules:

**1. One authority per fact.** Every fact has exactly one authoritative file. Everything else derives or points.

**2. Derived documents are never typed.** *Nothing that can be derived may be typed.* If it can be computed from the source, compute it.

**3. Pin the count, or don't state it.** A number stated in prose must be **re-measured** when the thing it counts changes — or stated timelessly.

**4. When two copies disagree, the receipt wins.** The authoritative recording beats the index.

> **The parser corollary:** a derived document must be regenerated, not hand-edited. If you find yourself editing generated output, you have found a defect in the generator.

**Source:** `library/FANFICTION_FRAMEWORK.md` §4.13 · `MISTAKES_LEDGER` · `soul-land-universal-kit` description

# PART 4 · THE GATES

## 4.1 The principle ⬛ UNIVERSAL

> **A chapter is not done when it reads well. It is done when it has been checked.**

And **a check that executes nothing verifies nothing.** If you cannot name the specific function, passage or code path the check actually touched, **you have not checked anything — you have re-read your own draft and felt reassured.**

> **A clean exit code is not a pass when the output is wrong. Read the output.**

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/09_AUDIT_LAW.md` §1

## 4.2 The seven hard gates ⬛ UNIVERSAL

Machine-checked. **These are not negotiable, and "mostly passing" is a failure.**

```
1. Zero characters in an unreadable script (CJK, kana, hangul) — not a blanket non-ASCII ban
2. Zero literal backslash-n sequences
3. Zero digits in prose (panels and ledgers exempt)
4. At least three spoken dialogue lines per chapter
5. Chapter panels contiguous — no overlap, no gap
6. No template or placeholder text left in a shipped file
7. The ◆ marker never appears in prose; at most one book-end card, opening
   with END OF, last in the file
```

> **Gate 4 exists because the dialogue register silently disappears from introspective serials.** It went missing from **seven of fifteen chapters** in one rebuild and was only caught by counting. **Count it; do not eyeball it.**
>
> **Gate 5 exists because** chapter insertion and reordering desynchronise panels, and the drift is invisible until two chapters claim the same year.

## 4.3 GATE SCOPE — *which gates apply to which files*

> **Getting this wrong produces a wall of false positives that trains everyone to ignore the gate. This was learned three separate times while building this kit.**

| Gate | Chapters | Templates | Law files | Codex / ledgers | Audit logs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 unreadable script | yes | yes | yes | yes | yes |
| 2 backslash-n | yes | yes | yes | yes | yes |
| 3 digits in prose | yes | — | — | no (ledgers hold figures) | no |
| 4 dialogue | yes | — | — | — | — |
| 5 contiguity | yes | — | — | — | — |
| 6 placeholders | **yes** | **NO** | **NO** | yes | **NO** |
| 7 marker discipline | yes | no | no | — | — |

> **Gate 6 must never be applied to templates, law files, or audit logs.** A template with no slot tokens is not a template, a law file that cannot show the panel format cannot teach it, and an audit log *reports on* placeholder counts — a line reading `TODO/FIXME/TBD = 0` is the log **doing its job**, not a leftover.
>
> **Gates 1 and 2 are the only ones that apply everywhere.** They are the ones that indicate a file is *broken* rather than a file *being what it is*.

## 4.4 The self-referential false positive ⬛ UNIVERSAL

Four times while building the kit, a gate fired on **the text that described the gate**:

1. A blanket non-ASCII gate flagged the em dashes in the prose law that explained the rule
2. A case-insensitive placeholder match flagged the word "placeholder" in a sentence saying "no placeholder text left"
3. Gate 6 applied to `templates/` flagged every slot token in every template
4. Gate 6 applied to an audit log flagged `TODO/FIXME/TBD = 0` — the log reporting it had found none

> **The general rule: a check must not match its own description.** If a gate fires on the documentation of the gate, **the gate is scoped wrong — not the documentation.** Fix the scope, because *"just reword the docs"* **leaves the trap armed for the next file.**

## 4.5 TESTING THE GATE ITSELF ⬛ UNIVERSAL — *the standard no repo in the account yet meets*

```
python3 tools/selftest.py
```

**Run this after every edit to the checker.** It builds throwaway chapters, each carrying exactly one known defect, and confirms every one is caught — then builds a clean chapter and confirms it passes.

> **Why this is not optional:** `verify.py` was once run against a proven 33,000-word serial and reported 15 failures. **All 15 were false positives from an over-broad rule. The serial was correct and the gate was wrong**, and nothing revealed that except a hand-built negative test.
>
> **A gate that has only ever been seen to pass is a gate nobody has tested.**

The self-test was itself mutation-tested: each gate was deliberately disabled in a copy of the kit, and the self-test failed every time. **A self-test that cannot fail is decoration.**

> **If you add an eighth gate, add an eighth fixture. A gate with no fixture is a gate you are hoping works.**

## 4.6 The verification pattern — the bug that passes everything ⬛ UNIVERSAL

```python
# WRONG (1) — the panel is not at file start; the title precedes it.
prose = re.sub(r'^```.*?```', '', s, flags=re.S)

# WRONG (2) — this one looks right and is not. Joining everything after the first
# fence reclassifies any LATER fenced block (a book-end card) as prose, so the
# marker gate and the digits gate both fire on apparatus they should ignore.
parts = s.split('```')
prose = ''.join(parts[2:])

# RIGHT — every fenced block is apparatus. Prose is only what sits outside all of
# them: the even indices past the first fence.
parts = s.split('```')
panel = parts[1] if len(parts) >= 3 else ''
prose = ''.join(parts[i] for i in range(2, len(parts), 2))
extra = [parts[i] for i in range(3, len(parts), 2)]   # book-end cards etc.
```

> **The second wrong version is the dangerous one.** It passes every test you think to write **until the day a chapter grows a second fenced block**, and then it produces failures that *look like real defects in the prose.* Both bugs were found in the kit's own gate, **on prose that was correct.**
>
> **That single distinction is the difference between a gate that catches digits in prose and a gate that quietly passes everything. A verification script with a bug in it is worse than no script, because it produces false confidence.**

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/09_AUDIT_LAW.md` §4

## 4.7 The Honesty Rule ⬛ UNIVERSAL

> **If the real check is blocked, unblocking it is part of the task.**

Install the missing dependency. Look past a bare `command -v` — the tool is often present but not on PATH. Prefer the project's own runner over a script you write yourself.

If it genuinely cannot be run, **say so plainly in the same breath as the change**: what you did, what you could not check, and why. That is an acceptable outcome and it costs nothing.

> **Presenting an unverified change as done is the failure to avoid. A guess written in the same voice as a verified fact is how you end up being wrong twice.**

## 4.8 THE FIVE MIS-MEASUREMENTS ⬛ UNIVERSAL

*The five things logged as "missing" that were already present:*

| # | Claimed missing | Reality |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | numbers were "footer-only" | they were spelled out in prose |
| 2 | the canon strength table was present | it was in headers only |
| 3 | a whole power system was "unused" | present in 9 chapters |
| 4 | a character's entrance was "missing" | the fight was there; only the arrival was absent |
| 5 | a canon growth mechanic was "never applied" | fully present, and used as a plot engine |

> **The single cause: searching for the words you expect instead of looking for the thing.**

### Countermeasures — all mandatory

1. **Scan for the CONCEPT across all chapters, not one keyword.** Try **three phrasings minimum**
2. **Check prose, headers and footers separately** — they are different things and get counted differently
3. **Numbers may be spelled out.** Grep for `two hundred and forty-three` as well as `243`
4. **Before logging a defect, try to disprove it.** Spend one call proving yourself wrong
5. **A checker can prove false presence. It cannot prove false absence. Only re-reading can**

**Source:** `library/FANFICTION_FRAMEWORK.md` §6

## 4.9 Calibrate a threshold before you obey it ⬛ UNIVERSAL

> **A check that cries wolf is worse than no check.**

Recalibration lesson: when a threshold produces false positives on correct work, the threshold is the defect — **fix the threshold, do not "fix" the prose.**

# PART 5 · AUDIT AND PROCESS

## 5.1 The five audits ⬛ UNIVERSAL

Run **all five** on every chapter, **in this order**. Repair at the smallest possible area, preserve established material, then record the new state.

| Audit | Location, timeline, present characters, knowledge, power levels, regional behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Canon** | Ask: **what changed *because of our divergence*?** Anything not deliberately changed remains canon. **An accidental change is a bug, not a liberty** |
| **Continuity** | Ages, dates, injuries, promises, relationships, impossible travel, resources. Cross-check every number against `CONTINUITY.md` |
| **Character** | Does the voice, the values and the decision-making still belong to this person? **Change must be earned.** A character who acts differently in ch14 than ch4 needs a visible reason, or one of the two chapters is wrong |
| **Power** | Did strength come from an established cause? What counters remain? Does the world react to what just became public? Has a reserved power been granted early? |
| **Tension** | What can be lost? Who can fail? What stays unknown? Why isn't this easy? **If all four answers are "nothing," the chapter has no stakes and should not ship** |

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/09_AUDIT_LAW.md` §2

## 5.2 When the audit finds something ⬛ UNIVERSAL

> **Fix it, then run the audit again. Not "note it and move on."**

**And check the blast radius.** One wrong date usually means the arithmetic around it is wrong too — the case that produced seventeen errors had a mantis living 43 years in one line and being alive 37 years later in another. **Fixing the first line without the second just moved the contradiction.**

> **When a class of error appears, sweep for the whole class. Do not fix the instance you happened to notice.**

## 5.3 Record the audit ⬛ UNIVERSAL

Every non-trivial audit gets a dated record in `audits/`:

```
audits/2026-09-18_TIMELINE_COMPRESSION.md
```

Contents: **what was found, what was wrong, what was changed, what was verified afterward, and what remains open.**

> **This is how a project survives a handoff. An agent who can read what was already caught does not re-litigate it.**

## 5.4 The per-chapter workflow ⬛ UNIVERSAL

*"UPDATE EVERYTHING, EVERY TIME" — author's standing directive*

```
pre-chapter board  →  draft  →  self-audit  →  gate  →  sync every file  →  ship
```

**Source:** `library/FANFICTION_FRAMEWORK.md` §8

## 5.5 THE SHIP LAW ⬛ UNIVERSAL

> **Mechanical 80% + authored 20%. Exit 0 only when every automated step is verified.**

A ship is split into:
- **the mechanical part** — the gate runs, zero failures, every file synced
- **the authored part** — the turn, the registers, the honesty check, the thing only a human can answer

**Exit 0 is claimed only when the automated half is genuinely green.** The authored half is recorded, not automated.

**Source:** `docs/01_the_laws.md` (Ship Law) · `soul-land-2-the-grey-wolf/tools/ship_chapter.py`

## 5.6 The correction discipline ⬛ UNIVERSAL

- **Verify causes still exist in the altered line** — never force canon back after a branch
- **Never restore conflicts via unexplained infrastructure**
- **Two-version pairs:** newest version canonical, elders kept for provenance
- **Rejection quarantine:** rejected material is filed, not deleted, and never re-enters the fact-space
- **Atomic assert discipline:** every patch asserts its own precondition, so a bad assumption fails loudly instead of quietly

# PART 6 · STRUCTURE AND HANDOFF

## 6.1 The file tree ⬛ UNIVERSAL

```
your_project/
├── README.md                    ← authority order, file map, live edge
├── foundation/
│   ├── STATUS_PANEL.md          ← THE single status source
│   ├── CONTINUITY.md            ← THE single continuity ledger
│   ├── NO_MISTAKE_LIVE_RULES.md ← the locks, current after chapter N
│   ├── CANON_NOTES.md           ← receipts and confidence tags
│   ├── CANON_LEDGER.md          ← every canon beat touched, and how
│   └── SERIAL_LOG.md            ← per-chapter record
├── codex/
│   ├── CHARACTERS.md
│   ├── TIMELINE.md
│   ├── PLACES.md
│   └── KNOWLEDGE_FIREWALLS.md
├── chapters/
│   └── Chapter_01_....md
└── audits/
    └── <date>_<subject>.md
```

> **Do not invent your own layout — the layout is what lets a fresh agent orient in one read.**

**Two rules about the tree:** one status source · filename law.

### What good setup looks like

**A finished setup is maybe 2,000 words across four files. It should take an afternoon.**

> **The failure mode is the opposite: 25,000 words of world bible before chapter one exists. That is procrastination with a file extension.** Set the twelve locks, write the panel, draft chapter one, and let the codex grow from things that actually happened on the page.
>
> **Nothing enters the codex until it happens in a chapter. A codex full of things that never appear is a lie you maintain at your own expense.**

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/02_PROJECT_SETUP.md`

## 6.2 Handoff Law ⬛ UNIVERSAL

> **A handoff is the highest-risk moment in a long serial. Every error that was fixed can be reintroduced by one agent reading one stale file.**

### The handoff file

One file at the project root, named `HANDOFF.md`. It must answer, **in order**:

```
1. WHAT THIS IS          — one paragraph. Era, protagonist, premise
2. THE LIVE EDGE         — the last chapter that counts, by number and title
3. THE STATUS SOURCE     — which file is authoritative. Name it
4. THE LOCKS             — the absolutes, verbatim
5. WHAT IS FORBIDDEN NOW — powers, reveals and events reserved for later
6. WHAT IS STALE         — superseded drafts, and where the live ones are
7. OPEN THREADS          — what is unresolved, and what each one needs
8. WHAT NOT TO RE-LITIGATE — decisions already made, with the reason
9. VERIFICATION          — the command to run, and what passing looks like
```

> **Keep it under 1,500 words. A handoff so long nobody reads it is worse than none.**

### Three defences against the stale-file problem

**Stamp them.** · **Say where the live ones are** — not just "the old ones are wrong," **name the directory that is current** · **Run the check first** — before writing anything, run the verification over the whole project and read the output. **Expect failures. Fix them before adding.**

### What a new agent does first — in this order

```
1. Read HANDOFF.md
2. Find the single status source. If two files claim to be current, STOP and resolve it
3. Find the live edge. Confirm which chapters count
4. Run the verification over everything. Read the output. Fix failures
5. Read the most recent audits/ record. Do not re-litigate settled decisions
6. Read the last chapter that counts
7. Only then plan the next chapter
```

> **Skipping to step 7 feels faster. It is much slower.**

### Include / do not include

**Include:** the locks **verbatim** — *do not paraphrase, paraphrases drift* · the exact verification command and what a pass looks like · the specific errors already caught, **so nobody rediscovers them** · anything the user explicitly ruled on, with the date.

**Do not include:** a plot summary (the chapters are the plot summary) · world lore (that lives in the codex) · your reasoning process (the audits carry what matters) · **anything you are unsure about, unmarked.** Mark it `[unverified]` or leave it out.

## 6.3 The user's words are the top layer ⬛ UNIVERSAL

When a user has ruled on something, **record it verbatim with the date.**

```
User ruling (2026-09-08): Blue Silver Valley IS canon Soul Land 1.
Never "[design]", never "canon nowhere names it". Do not re-add revoked wording.
```

> **Paraphrased rulings get re-argued. Verbatim rulings with dates don't.**
>
> **And when a ruling is revoked, record the revocation too, with its own date.** Otherwise a future agent finds the original ruling, believes it, and **undoes the correction.**

## 6.4 Cross-project contamination ⬛ UNIVERSAL

If you run more than one serial in the same franchise, **they will bleed into each other.**

Every project needs its own: status source · continuity ledger · lock file · numbering.

> **Numbers never cross.** Protagonist A's rank is not protagonist B's rank. Two serials in the same world **share canon; they share nothing else.**

> **And identify a project from its own contents** before applying another project's warnings to it. A file labelled "do not open" in project A may be pointing at a completely different file in project B. **Check the magic bytes, read the header, confirm the franchise — then decide.**

**Source:** `SOUL_LAND_UNIVERSAL_KIT/10_HANDOFF_LAW.md`

# PART 11 · THE ONE-PAGE VERSION

*If you read nothing else:*

**Before you write anything**
Set the twelve locks. Fill the spine sentence — *"\_\_\_ wants \_\_\_ from my protagonist, and will \_\_\_ to get it."* Name five canon beats you touch. Write the panel. **Nothing enters the codex until it happens on a page.**

**Before every chapter**
Ask the seven questions. Set the panel endpoints **first**, then derive every date.

**While writing**
≥3 registers · ≥3 spoken dialogue lines · no digits in prose · plain words a stranger parses on one reading · open in scene · one line of consequence, never a summary · **write it once.**

**After writing**
Run the five audits in order: canon, continuity, character, power, tension. Run the gate — **and have tested the gate on known-bad input first.** Fix what it finds, then run it again. Sweep the whole class of error, not the instance.

**The three sentences that carry the most weight**

> **A chapter is not done when it reads well. It is done when it has been checked.**
> **A check that executes nothing verifies nothing.**
> **A gate that has only ever been seen to pass is a gate nobody has tested.**

**The one that protects the story**

> **Consistency > convenience. Causality > coincidence. Character agency > author control. Earned progression > arbitrary escalation. Consequences > spectacle. Truth > memory.**
